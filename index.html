The Music

The voice behind
the vinyl

VYNL started as a late-night notebook and a bedroom mic. What began as scattered thoughts and looped melodies turned into something bigger — a space where music and writing collide, where every track has a story and every post has a pulse.

This blog is my home base. Here you will find deep dives into the craft, behind-the-scenes breakdowns of my tracks, and honest writing about the sounds that shaped me. No gatekeeping. No algorithms. Just music, words, and the space between them.

Every track on this site is original. Every lyric is mine. Hit play, read the words, and if something resonates — stick around.
